Sori Bloom is a personal preview of a gentler, local-first budget companion.

Download **sori-bloom-debug.apk** directly on your Android tablet and open it to install. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or files app. Supports Android 10 and newer. It installs separately from the original Sori app.

This preview includes expense and income logging, budgets, calendar, forecasts, savings goals, debt planning, a pause list, local coaching and opt-in reminders. Fresh setup starts with an empty personal ledger. Sample preview is separate from your personal data.

The APK is the exact previously built and signature-verified personal debug package, not an APK re-signed with a new CI debug key. Its SHA-256 file is included. Back up your records before updating; long-term releases need a persistent signing key. This preview has not been tested on your physical tablet.

**sori-bloom-web.zip** contains the compiled static web app for manual hosting. It does not include private data or deploy a Cloudflare Worker. Cloud sync, remote MCP and optional cloud AI require your own configured backend. Publishing this preview does not deploy anything to Cloudflare, enable paid AI, or include your passphrase.

ChatGPT and Claude subscriptions do not supply general API credits. Local budgeting and local suggestions work without an AI key. Read the repository's deployment and AI guides before enabling remote services.
